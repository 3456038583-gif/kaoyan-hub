作文模板最终版

核心词汇

  • 图表：figure, chart, diagram, graph
    • table，line chart，bar chart，pie chart
  • 图画：illustration, figure, cartoon, drawing, picture, image

第一段: 图画/图表描述 (Description)

1 图画

1.1 首句：描述

  • 单张图无对比

    • As is symbolically depicted in the figure/diagram/drawing above， +简单句描述环境/基本动作，passionate/confiden/disgracefult as well as aggressive/optimistic/pessimistic，+主要动作

  • 单张图有对比/双图/一图一表

    1. As is symbolically depicted in the figure/diagram/drawing above, +简单句描述环境/基本动作, in which the actions/attitudes/mentalities of the two characters form a very ironic and stark contrast: - In the left , +描述图片 deems/considers/holds; - In the right , +描述图片.
    2. The drawing and the diagram briefly portrays the fact that +句子总结事实描述. - In the left , +描述图片 deems/considers/holds; - In the right , +描述图片.

1.2 主题阐释句

  • 图片有句子

    • Below/Above the image, there is a very prominent sentence that roughly says: +图片下/上面的句子.

  • 图片无句子

    1. The cartoon succinctly/undoubtedly encapsulates the profound/dual essence/nature/paradox of +关键词 and its implications, + which - is a guiding light to help us confront a myriad of challenges - attempts to alert the whole society that
    2. Simple as the drawing is, it conveys a truth that the utmost importance ought to be attached to [核心词] by the whole society.

2 图表

2.1 第一句：总体描述+细节描述

  • 动态图：
    • As is plainly exhibited in the diagram/bar chart above，the 主题词 witnessed significant changes from 年份 to 年份
    • To be specific，Sth1. climb/rocket/surge remarkably/see a remarkble climb from 数量 to 数量. In the meanwhile, Sth2. diminish/shrink/see a slight increase from 数量 to 数量 during the same period.
  • 静态图:
    • The bar chart reflects the distribution/composition of 主题词 at a glance, revealing a striking disparity/diversity.
    • To be specific，the 主题词1、2、3 account for/make up/the proportion of xx% respectively, Especially, 主题词1 ranks the top.

2.2 第二局：过渡句引出+主题阐述

  • It is apparent from the statistics that the landscape of 主题词 has been evolving rapidly in recent years

3 文字 : 文字改写+表明态度

  • Nowadays, it is well-known that the problem/ phenomenon of 主题词 has caused wide concern.
  • While/Therefore, the author holds that
  • I (don't) agree with the author’s point of view to some extent

第二段: 原因分析/意义阐述

2.1 首句：承上启下

  • 方案 A (感性/强逻辑 - 推荐图画)
    • Not until I see the drawing do I realize the magnitude of [核心词]. The fundamental factors can be explored from the following perspectives.
  • 方案 B (理性/学术风 - 推荐图表)
    • There exist a myriad of determinants accounting for this phenomenon, which can be explored from the following perspectives.

2.2 中间：分点论述/正反论证

  • 论点一 (强调价值/功能)

    • To begin with, definitely no one can deny such an obvious fact that [核心词]
      1. acts as the catalyst for/engine that /bridge between/foundation upon which [五大方面]
      2. play a vital/ exceedingly crucial / an indispensable role in [五大方面]
    • Not only does it
      1. indicate a significant rise
      2. facilitate/enhancee/deepens friendship and kinship
      3. lay the foundation of
    • but it also
      1. demonstrates that
      2. foster our virtue/cultivate a positive mindset
      3. enables

  • 论点二 (反面假设/重要性)

    • Furthermore, the significance of [核心词] cannot be overstated. It serves as an indispensable instrument for us to [五大方面]. If we failed to [重视/解决该问题]，needless to say,
      1. our society would be confronted with an unpromising and fruitless future.
      2. may undermine
      3. constitute a significant menace to

第三段: 结论与升华

3.1 通用：总结观点+ 多方行动+展望升华

  • Given the arguments above/on balance,
    1. we can come to the conclusion that [核心词] is not an option, but a necessity.
    2. Under no circumstance should we cease our pursuit of a more convenient and intelligent lifestyle.
  • For one thing, it is imperative for us to take joint efforts.
  • For another,
    • mass media, such as radios, televisions, newspapers and the Internet, should appeal to people to
    • authorities should formulate policies to [支持/监管], while mass media should spare no pains to [宣传/引导].
  1. By embracing these values/ways (which will serve as the guiding light), I am convinced that we will transcend the passage of time and embrace a brighter future.
  2. Only through the joint efforts of all people can we effectively solve the problem/ have a better life/ push the social progress

3.2 图表特化：总结全文+趋势预测

  • Given the arguments above/on balance, in the next few years/ we may predict that
    1. an increasing number of people may focus on/ choose to
    2. the [核心词] will also witness larger-scale/faster-paced/higher-quality development.
  • and this trend will not change in the near future

考研英语写作素材库：五大方面

persistence/perseverance/tenacity 坚持； introspection 反思； integrity 诚信；a positive mindset 积极心态；critical thinking 批判性思维； cooperation/collaboration/teamwork 合作；moral principles/ethics 道德准则； technology 科技；entrepreneurship 创业精神

🏙️ 一、社会方面 (Society)

核心是发展 (Development)
  • social progress/ development/ stability：社会进步/发展/稳定
  • sustained and healthy growth：持续健康发展
  • high-quality development：高质量发展

[!example]- 方式是经济 (Economy)

  • rapid economic growth：快速经济增长
  • economic prosperity：经济繁荣
  • rural revitalization：农村振兴
  • expand the market：开拓市场
  • industrial upgrading：产业升级
  • high-end manufacturing：高端制造
  • employment promotion：促进就业
  • promote consumption：促进消费
  • disposable income：可支配收入

[!tip]- 特征是服务 (Service)

  • public services：公共服务
  • bring convenience to life：生活便利
  • public health security：公共卫生安全
  • infrastructure development：基础设施建设
  • social security system：社保体系
  • elderly care services：养老服务

[!success]- 目标是和谐 (Harmony)

  • social harmony：社会和谐
  • prosperity of the country：国家富强
  • happiness of the people：人民幸福
  • social cohesion：社会凝聚力
  • social justice：社会公正
  • social morality：社会公德
  • common prosperity：共同富裕

🌲 二、自然方面 (Nature)

核心是持续 (Sustainability)
  • maintain natural ecological balance：维持自然生态平衡
  • sustainable development：可持续发展

[!example]- 方式是保护 (Protection)

  • improve ecological environment：改善生态环境
  • natural resource preservation：自然资源保护
  • prevent and remedy pollution：预防污染
  • green economy：绿色经济
  • develop renewable energy：发展可再生能源
  • waste classification and recycling：垃圾分类与回收

[!success]- 目标是和谐 (Harmony)

  • comfortable climate：舒适的气候
  • beautiful natural environment：优美的自然环境
  • peaceful coexistence：和平共处
  • harmony between humanity and nature：人与自然和谐共生

🏮 三、文化方面 (Culture)

核心是传承 (Inheritance)
  • inherit and develop the traditional culture：继承和发扬传统文化
  • cultural protection：文化保护
  • digital preservation：数字化保护

[!example]- 方式是交流 (Exchange)

  • cultural exchange：文化交流
  • people-to-people exchange：人文交流
  • organize cultural exchange exhibitions：组织文化交流展览
  • enjoy and learn from historical sites：享受和学习历史古迹
  • study/ understand/ respect history：学习/了解/尊重历史

[!tip]- 态度是包容 (Inclusion)

  • diversity and inclusion：多元化与包容性
  • open and inclusive attitude：开放和包容的态度
  • respect different customs：尊重不同的风俗

[!success]- 目标是繁荣 (Prosperity)

  • promotion of cultural diversity：促进文化多样性
  • cultural confidence：文化自信
  • cultural identity：文化认同
  • cultural soft power：文化软实力

🏃 四、个人方面 (Individual)

核心是成长 (Growth)
  • personal growth：个人成长
  • youth development：青年发展
  • the all-round development：全面发展

[!quote]- 基础是健康 (Health)

  • physical and mental health：身体和心理健康
  • mental well-being：心理健康
  • relieve stress：缓解压力
  • alleviate difficulties：缓解困难
  • healthy lifestyle：健康生活方式
  • keep one's mind sharp：保持思维敏锐
  • foster a positive and dynamic mindset：培养积极有活力的心态
  • quality of life：生活质量
  • psychological quality：心理素质
  • self-protection awareness：自我保护意识

[!example]- 方式是学习 (Learning)

  • expand our horizons：开拓眼界
  • broaden our experience：丰富经验
  • acquire knowledge/ skill/ ability：获得知识/技巧/能力
  • give full play to our advantages：发挥自身优势
  • embrace challenges：迎接挑战
  • promote one's self-confidence：提升自信
  • lifelong education and learning：终身教育和学习

[!success]- 目标是实现 (Realization)

  • personal goals and values：个人目标与价值
  • moral cultivation：道德修养
  • enrich spiritual life：丰富精神生活
  • enhance individual well-being：提升个人福祉
  • self-realization/ self-fulfillment：自我实现

🤝 五、交流方面 (Communication)

核心是沟通 (Communication)
  • facilitate effective communication：促进有效沟通
  • promote the efficiency of communication：提高交流效率

[!example]- 方式是连接 (Connection)

  • bridge communication gaps：弥合沟通鸿沟
  • establish communication bridges：建立沟通桥梁
  • effective communication tools：有效的沟通工具
  • collect/gather information：搜集信息
  • shrink the distance：缩短距离

[!success]- 目标是和谐 (Harmony)

  • facilitate information sharing：促进信息共享
  • enhance mutual understanding：增进相互理解
  • deepen friendship/ kinship：增进友谊/亲情
  • build consensus：建立共识
  • drive social progress：推动社会进步
