2026英一大小作文预测与检验

基于你提供的历年真题 PDF（特别是注意到 2025 年英语一罕见地考查了图表/表格，打破了二十多年的图画传统）以及当前的日期（2025 年 12 月 18 日，即考前两天），以下是针对**2026 考研英语一（考试日期：2025 年 12 月 20 日）**的终极预测与作文模版实战。

一、 2026 考研英语一 · 终极预测

1. 小作文预测 (Small Composition)

由于 2024 年和 2025 年连续两年考查了书信（建议/回复信），根据“冷门回补”和“题型轮换”原则，今年**通知（Notice）**的概率无限大。

  • No. 1 预测：通知 (Notice)
    • 内容： 招募志愿者（国际会议、支教、环保活动）、举办讲座/比赛通知。
    • 理由： 考研英语一的传统强项，且已隔两年未考。
  • No. 2 预测：推荐信 (Letter of Recommendation)
    • 内容： 推荐一本书、一部电影、一个旅游景点或中国传统文化。
    • 理由： 经典功能性书信，适合套用准备好的素材。
  • No. 3 预测：邀请信 (Letter of Invitation)
    • 内容： 邀请外教做评委、邀请同学参加聚会。

2. 大作文预测 (Big Composition)

重磅分析： PDF 显示 2025 年英语一考查了“表格（Table）”，这是极罕见的情况。根据命题规律，2026 年极大概率会回归最传统的“图画作文（Drawing/Cartoon）”，以维持难度和题型的稳定性。

  • No. 1 核心话题：人工智能与独立思考 (AI & Independent Thinking)
    • 场景： 学生让 AI 写作业 vs 苦读的学生；人们沉溺手机 vs 读书的人。
    • 寓意： 科技是双刃剑，不能丧失独立思考能力。
  • No. 2 核心话题：坚持与行动 (Persistence & Action)
    • 场景： 两个人面对困难，一个空谈一个行动；或者挖井的漫画。
    • 寓意： 拒绝焦虑，脚踏实地。
  • No. 3 核心话题：传统文化与现代融合 (Traditional Culture)
    • 场景： 传统艺术（如京剧、汉服）在现代社会的流行或被忽视。

二、大作文实战演练

预测题目： Directions: Write an essay of 160-200 words based on the following drawing. In your essay, you should

  1. describe the drawing briefly,
  2. interpret its intended meaning, and
  3. give your comments.

设定图画内容： 两幅图对比。 左图：一个学生躺着睡觉，旁边一个机器人（写着 ChatGPT/AI）在帮他写论文。 右图：一个学生在灯下苦读思考，写得很慢但很认真。 文字说明： “省力不等于能力”（Labor-saving is not ability）或 “别让大脑生锈”（Don't let your brain rust）。

【根据你提供的模版撰写的范文】

Title: The Paradox of Technology and Intelligence

(第一段：图画描述) As is symbolically depicted in the drawing above, the actions of the two characters form a very ironic and stark contrast: In the left, a young student is sound asleep while a robot is busy writing his thesis, acting as a "ghostwriter"; In the right, another student is burying his head in books, sweating and thinking hard to complete his task independently. Below the image, there is a very prominent sentence that roughly says: "Technology makes life easier, but thinking makes us stronger."

(第二段：原因分析/意义阐述) Not until I see the drawing do I realize the magnitude of independent thinking in this digital era. The fundamental factors that contribute to the above-mentioned tendency may be interpreted as follows. To begin with, definitely no one can deny such an obvious fact that critical thinking acts as the foundation upon which human innovation and social progress are built. Not only does it facilitate a deep understanding of knowledge, but it also demonstrates the unique value of human wisdom that cannot be simply mimicked by codes. Furthermore, the significance of mental exertion cannot be overstated. It serves as an indispensable instrument for us to cultivate a resilient mind. If we failed to cherish the ability to think independently, needless to say, our society would be confronted with an unpromising and fruitless future where humans might become slaves to algorithms.

(第三段：结论与升华) Given the arguments above, we can come to the conclusion that maintaining independent thinking is not an option, but a necessity. Under no circumstance should we cease our pursuit of wisdom and truth, even when technology offers a shortcut. For one thing, it is imperative for us to take joint efforts to use tools wisely rather than relying on them. For another, mass media should spare no pains to appeal to young people to balance digital convenience with intellectual development. By embracing these values, I am convinced that we will transcend the passage of time and embrace a brighter future.

三、模版使用小贴士 (Tips)

  1. 第一段的“Left/Right”： 如果考题是一幅画（比如 2025 年之前的大多数年份），就用模版中的 Simple as the drawing is... 或 As is symbolically depicted in the drawing above... 直接描述动作，不用写左右对比。
  2. 核心词替换： 文中的 [核心词] (如 independent thinking, critical thinking) 是得分关键。如果是环保题材，就换成 environmental protection；如果是文化题材，就换成 traditional culture。
  3. 时态注意： 描述图画用一般现在时或现在进行时；中间论述用一般现在时。
  4. 字数控制： 这套模版字数非常充足，大约在 180-200 词之间，非常符合英语一的要求。考试时注意书写工整。
