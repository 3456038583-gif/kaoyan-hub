#线代 #矩阵 #转置矩阵 别名：转置矩阵

将 m×nm \times nm×n 矩阵 A=(aij)m×nA=(a_{ij})_{m \times n}A=(aij​)m×n​ 的行与列互换得到的 n×mn \times mn×m 矩阵，称为矩阵 AAA 的转置矩阵，记为 ATA^TAT，即

AT=[a11a21⋯am1a12a22⋯am2⋮⋮⋮a1na2n⋯amn] A^T = \begin{bmatrix} a_{11} & a_{21} & \cdots & a_{m1} \\ a_{12} & a_{22} & \cdots & a_{m2} \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ a_{1n} & a_{2n} & \cdots & a_{mn} \end{bmatrix} AT=​a11​a12​⋮a1n​​a21​a22​⋮a2n​​⋯⋯⋯​am1​am2​⋮amn​​​

转置矩阵满足下列运算规律：

  1. (AT)T=A(A^T)^T=A(AT)T=A
  2. (kA)T=kAT(kA)^T=kA^T(kA)T=kAT
  3. (A+B)T=AT+BT(A+B)^T=A^T+B^T(A+B)T=AT+BT
  4. (AB)T=BTAT(AB)^T=B^TA^T(AB)T=BTAT
