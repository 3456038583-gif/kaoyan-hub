    Δz=f(x0+Δx,y0+Δy)−f(x0,y0)=AΔx+BΔy+o(ρ) \Delta z = f(x_0 + \Delta x, y_0 + \Delta y) - f(x_0, y_0) = A\Delta x + B\Delta y + o(\rho) Δz=f(x0​+Δx,y0​+Δy)−f(x0​,y0​)=AΔx+BΔy+o(ρ)
